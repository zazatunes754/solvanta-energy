and served locally (no hot-linking). Sources, licences and credits are
documented below.

Notes on this collection:

- Downloaded from the Wikimedia Commons thumbnail service, then re-encoded as
  high-quality progressive JPEGs (quality 80, mozjpeg) with downscaling where
  the source was oversized (the home hero ships at 2400 px). Retouched copies
  can be regenerated with `node scripts/process-images.mjs <source> <name> [width]`
  (requires the `sharp` dev dependency).
- Attribution is carried where the image is used prominently: a small
  always-visible credit sits in the top-right corner of the home hero, and
  photographer/licence credits appear in visible captions on the homepage
  editorial section, estimator, contact page and solutions gallery.
- The Canberra aerial series frames different streets, so the remaining aerial
  placements read as distinct photographs; article covers no longer repeat the
  same photograph across two articles (the incentives and battery-capacity
  covers were moved to dedicated photographs in October 2026).
- The 2026-10 photography pass added four location-verified photographs
  (Corinda 2021 hero, West End tiled roof, Kawana street array and Kawana
  metal roof) and replaced the hero; responsive WebP/JPEG variants for every
  photo are generated by `node scripts/build-image-variants.mjs`.

## Re-downloading an image

Pexels image URLs follow the pattern
`https://images.pexels.com/photos/{id}/pexels-photo-{id}.jpeg?auto=compress&cs=tinysrgb&w=1600&fm=webp`.

Wikimedia Commons files can be re-downloaded (at any pixel width) with
`https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Special:FilePath/{filename}?width={width}`.

For example, to refresh the hero image:

```powershell
Invoke-WebRequest -Uri "https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Special:FilePath/Suburban%20rooftop%20solar%20power%20generation%20in%20Canberra%2C%20Australia.jpg?width=2400" -OutFile "public\images\canberra-solar-suburb-hero.jpg"
```

After replacing any image, check the `alt` text (in `src/data/gallery.ts`,
`src/data/services.ts`, `src/data/articles.ts` and the page components) still
describes the photo accurately — accessibility depends on it.

## Fonts

The site loads **Fraunces**, **Manrope** and **JetBrains Mono** from Google
Fonts (all under the SIL Open Font License). If you prefer zero
third-party requests at runtime, self-host the font files and update the
`<link>` tags in `index.html` — the font stack in
`src/styles/tokens.css` already falls back to system fonts.
